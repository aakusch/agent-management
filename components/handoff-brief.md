Write the closing brief for Aaron.

- What the meeting asked for, in one sentence, with the attribution.
- What changed, by file.
- What was verified, with the command and its result. Never claim a check you did not run.
- The PR URL and the branch it targets.
- What is still open: unanswered questions, deferred scope, anything that needs his decision.
- The single next action you recommend.

Rank by consequence. Keep it to what he needs to act on.
