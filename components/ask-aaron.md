Ask the open questions and wait.

Present, in order of consequence: each question, why it blocks, and the options you see with your
recommendation. Keep it to what actually blocks progress — do not relitigate settled scope.

Then stop. Do not proceed on an assumption. Record the answers verbatim and pass them forward as
part of the request.
